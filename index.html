Golf Score App — Privacy Notice

Personal, independently-developed app. Last updated 2026-09-04.

What the app stores, and where

On your device only

  • Your rounds, scores, per-hole shot detail, and settings are stored locally on your phone and work fully offline.
  • Live GPS distance-to-green and the tap-to-measure tool are computed on the device from your location and are never sent anywhere.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

Sign-in is entirely optional; everything above works without it. When you do sign in:

  • Your name, email address, and profile picture URL come from your Google account (via the verified sign-in token) and are stored on the backend so your rounds and settings sync across your own devices.
  • Your rounds sync to the backend under your account.
  • Player discovery. So that a playing partner can link your scores to your account, other signed-in users can look you up:
    • by email — exact match only; on by default (this is how player linking has always worked);
    • by name — exact whole-name match only, no partial/prefix search; off by default, opt in under Settings › Privacy.
    With both off, nobody can add you as a linked player — your rounds stay solo. You can still add and link other players yourself either way.
  • A player you link into a round can see that player entry's name, scores, and any shot detail recorded for them during that round. In a live (shared) round, participants see each other's names, avatars, and running scores.

Shot locations (only if you enable "track shot locations")

  • The GPS positions you played off-green shots from are saved to the backend alongside the round. This is the only location data that leaves your device, and only when you turn the setting on.
  • Putts are never location-tracked.

Home club

If you set a home club, the club name is stored with your synced settings and shown in player-search results as a disambiguator.

Retention & deletion

  • Deleting a round in the app removes it from the backend too.
  • Signing out stops syncing and clears the local account/session.
  • Settings › Privacy › Delete account permanently removes your account, synced settings, and every round you own — server-side and on that device. Rounds other people own that you joined stay theirs, with your link removed. Not reversible.
  • Settings › Privacy › Export my data returns everything the backend holds about you.

Contact

This is an independently-developed hobby app, not run by a company. For questions about your data, use the in-app "Export my data" / "Delete account" controls in Settings › Privacy.
