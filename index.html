Purple Martin — Privacy Notice

Purple Martin is a golf app made in Finland. Data controller: Samuli Lappalainen, Finland (contact@purplemartingolf.com). Last updated 2026-10-06.

Terms of Service · Käyttöehdot

Questions or requests: contact@purplemartingolf.com. This notice says what the app does with your data — nothing more happens to it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking SDKs in the app.

On your device

  • Your rounds, scores and settings are stored on your phone (and watch) and work fully offline. Signed out, they stay there — nothing about your rounds is uploaded.
  • Live distances to the green and tap-to-measure are worked out on your device and never leave it.
  • Battery use. The battery level, screen-on time and GPS use of your phone and watches are noted for each round and kept only on your devices (the phone keeps the last 10 rounds). A short summary goes into the app log, so it leaves your phone only if you attach the log to feedback.
  • Help improve shot detection (off unless you say yes — the app asks once, at your first round with a watch; change it any time in Settings › Watch): your Wear OS or Garmin watch records its GPS track and wrist motion during rounds and passes the file to your phone. When you're signed in, the phone sends it with that round's scorecard and marked shots to Purple Martin's server, only to improve shot detection. Legal basis: your consent. Kept 12 months, deleted with your account; a copy stays on your phone for 90 days, or until you sign out.

Finding courses

  • Courses near me, and starting a round on the watch, send your position rounded to about 100 m to our backend. The backend snaps it to a 500 m square and asks golfapi.io for nearby courses from the square's centre, so your own position never leaves our backend. The courses found around that square are kept for 30 days to answer the next search there, linked to no one.
  • Course searches (the text you type) go the same way.

Weather and elevation

  • Plays-like distance (temperature) and the wind line use the weather at the course. The app asks our backend by course only; the backend asks MET Norway (Open-Meteo if MET Norway is unavailable) for the course's location. Your position is never sent.
  • Height data for plays-like and green slopes is fetched by our backend for the course area only: from the National Land Survey of Finland, Estonia's Maa- ja Ruumiamet, Portugal's DGT (tiles downloaded once and stored on our server), and elsewhere Open-Meteo.
  • On a Garmin watch, if the phone has sent no weather, the watch uses Garmin's own weather.

Crash reports

  • If the Android phone app or the Wear OS watch app crashes, a report is sent to our backend the next time the app opens: the error and where in the code it happened, the app version, and the phone or watch model and Android version. It carries a random ID for this installation of the app — not your account, name or location — and is used only to fix the bug.
  • The Garmin watch app sends no crash reports.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

Everything above works without an account. If you sign in:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture come from your Google account and are stored so your rounds and settings follow you across your devices.
  • Your rounds are stored under your account, including the players you add to them (their name, gender, handicap and scores) and any shot detail you record.
  • Your settings sync too: handicap index, gender, units, home club, the clubs in your bag and your saved buddies.
  • Player lookup. So a playing partner can link your scores to your account, other signed-in users can find you by your name or the start of it, e.g. the first three letters (on by default), or by exact email (off by default) — Settings › Player discovery. A result shows your name, picture and home club; your handicap index only when they typed your full name.
  • Playing together. A player you link into your round can see that entry's name, scores and shot detail. In a live round the participants see each other's names, pictures and scores — not your shot locations. In an event, your name and score appear on that event's leaderboard.
  • Your own photo and banner (optional). If you upload a profile photo or a cover banner, it is stored on Purple Martin's server (Microsoft Azure, EU) with location and other hidden data removed, and shown instead of your Google picture to golfers who can already see you: people in your rounds and events, your buddies, and player search. Remove it any time in Settings; it is deleted with your account.
  • Reports and blocks. If you report someone's picture, the report (your account, the picture, the reason and any note) is kept to handle it and for up to 12 months after it's resolved; the person reported isn't told who reported. The players you block are stored with your account. Both are included in Export my data. Deleting your account removes your name from reports you filed and deletes reports about you. Pictures are reviewed by the developer; no outside moderation service is used. A picture reported by three different golfers is hidden until it has been reviewed.
  • Your devices. Each device you use while signed in is listed under your account (model, Android and app version, first and last use), shown to you in Settings › Your devices.
  • Send feedback (Settings, or the round menu) sends what you write, with your name and email so we can reply, the app version and phone model, any screenshots you add (from the round menu, a picture of the screen you were on is added first — you can remove it), and — only if “Attach the app log” is on — the app's activity log from recent days, which includes courses, scores, sync events and approximate locations (to about 1 km) during rounds. Kept for up to 12 months, or until you delete your account, and used only to fix problems and improve the app. The log stays on your phone unless you send it.

Shot locations (only if you turn on “Track shot locations”)

  • The spots you played off-green shots from are saved with the round under your account — also on rounds you join on someone else’s phone. Only you get them back. Putts are never location-tracked.

Your data, your choice — including deleting your account

  • Delete a round in the app and it is removed from our backend too.
  • Settings › Privacy › Export my data gives you everything the backend holds about you.
  • Settings › Privacy › Export my rounds makes a spreadsheet (CSV) of the rounds on this phone and shares it only with the app you pick — nothing is sent to us. Works signed out too.
  • Settings › Privacy › Delete account permanently removes your account, settings and every round you own, on the server and on that device. Rounds other people own that you joined stay theirs, with your link removed.
  • Server logs (which device called which part of the backend, and when) are kept for up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting, then deleted automatically. They are not removed by deleting your account.
  • Signing out stops syncing and ends the session on that device; your rounds stay on it.

Delete your Purple Martin account without the app

Email contact@purplemartingolf.com from the address of the Google account you signed in with, and ask for your account to be deleted. Your account, settings and every round you own are deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it's done.
