Birdie Book — Terms of Service

Birdie Book is a free golf scorecard and GPS distance app for Android phones and Wear OS watches, made in Finland by Samuli Lappalainen ("we", "us"). Contact: birdiebook.golf.app@gmail.com.

By using the app you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use it. How we handle your data is explained separately in the privacy notice.

1. The service

  • The app is free. There are no ads and nothing to buy.
  • It's a hobby project, provided as is. We try to keep it working well, but we can't promise it will always be available, error-free, or that every feature will stay.
  • Most features work without an account. Syncing, live rounds with other players and events need you to sign in with a Google account.

2. Distances, maps and handicaps are estimates

  • GPS distances, course maps, hazard reach and carry, and "plays like" figures come from your device's GPS and from course data we don't control (golfapi.io and OpenStreetMap). They can be wrong — sometimes by a lot, for example with a poor GPS signal or an out-of-date map. Use your own judgement, and never rely on the app where a mistake could put you or anyone else at risk.
  • The calculated Handicap Index is our estimate using the World Handicap System method on the rounds in the app. It is not your official handicap and can't replace the one kept by your club or national federation.

3. Using the app in competitions

Whether a distance-measuring device is allowed is up to the competition. Under the Rules of Golf (Rule 4.3) distance information is allowed only where the committee permits it by local rule, and features that measure elevation changes — the app's "plays like" (slope-adjusted) distance — must be switched off. Checking the rules and setting the app up accordingly is your responsibility.

4. Your account

  • Sign in only with your own Google account, and keep your devices reasonably secure — anyone who can use your signed-in phone can use your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings › Privacy › Delete account, or by emailing us (see the privacy notice).

5. What you put in the app

  • Your rounds, scores and settings are yours. You give us permission to store and process them only as needed to run the app for you — to sync them to your devices and show them to the people you play with, as the privacy notice describes. We don't sell them or use them for advertising.
  • Other people's details. When you add players to a round, buddies to your list, or create an event, only use names and handicaps of people who are fine with you doing so.
  • Events. If you create an event, you're responsible for running it: who you share the code with, and what the event is called. Event names and player names appear to everyone in the event.

6. Fair use

Don't:

  • use offensive, hateful or misleading names for yourself, players or events;
  • use player search or events to collect other users' details, or to harass anyone;
  • try to break, overload or get around the limits of the app or its backend, or access data that isn't yours;
  • copy the course data out of the app in bulk (our course data provider doesn't allow it to be passed on).

We may remove content or suspend an account that breaks these rules. Where it's reasonable, we'll tell you first.

7. Other people's data and services

  • Course data: golfapi.io. Maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors (Open Database License), map tiles by MapTiler. These are provided by others; we can't guarantee their accuracy or availability.
  • Signing in uses Google; Google's own terms apply to your Google account.

8. Changes and ending the service

  • We may update the app and these terms. If a change matters, we'll say so in the app or on this page before it takes effect. The date at the bottom shows the latest version.
  • We may end the service. If we do, we'll give notice in the app where we can, and you'll have time to export your data (Settings › Privacy › Export my data).
  • You can stop using the app whenever you like, and delete your account.

9. Liability

The app is free and provided as is. To the extent the law allows, we're not liable for losses caused by using it or by not being able to use it — for example a wrong distance, a lost score, or a missed round. Nothing in these terms limits your rights as a consumer under Finnish or EU law, or our liability where the law doesn't allow it to be limited.

10. Law and disputes

These terms are governed by Finnish law. If something goes wrong, please email us first — we'd much rather fix it. As a consumer you can also take a dispute to the Finnish Consumer Disputes Board (Kuluttajariitalautakunta) or the courts of your home country.

Golfin sääntöjen (sääntö 4.3) mukaan etäisyystietoa saa käyttää vain, jos kilpailutoimikunta sallii sen paikallissäännöllä, ja korkeuserot huomioivan toiminnon – sovelluksen "plays like" -etäisyyden – on oltava pois päältä.
